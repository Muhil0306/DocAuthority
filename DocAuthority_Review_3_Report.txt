1.1 PROBLEM STATEMENT & CORE INNOVATION
----------------------------------------
Enterprise knowledge is fragmented across PDFs, chat records, meeting 
transcripts, and email records. Employees frequently rely on unapproved draft 
versions due to standard search engines naively returning files based only on 
newest modified dates.

DocAuthority resolves this by implementing a Multi-Factor Authority Resolver 
Algorithm:
  - Approval Status Score  : Max 50 Points (APPROVED=50, PENDING=20, DRAFT=10)
  - Department Ownership   : Max 25 Points (verified owner bonus)
  - Chronological Recency  : Max 25 Points (version update order)

1.2 RECAP OF REVIEW 2 ENHANCEMENTS
----------------------------------
- Heterogeneous Ingestion : 4 channels (PDFs, Slack/Teams Chats, Transcripts, Emails).
- Benchmark Corpus        : 105 Documents, 312 Versions, 50 Labeled Queries.
